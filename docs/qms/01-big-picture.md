# QMS Big Picture

1. A Quality Management System (QMS) is the set of documented procedures and records a pharma company uses to prove that every product is made consistently, safely and traceably (GMP: "if it isn't documented, it didn't happen").
2. In the supply chain, a defect in an API or raw material batch can reach millions of patients, so every quality event has to be recorded, investigated and closed.
3. Deviation and CAPA cover problems inside manufacturing: a deviation records an unplanned departure from procedure, and CAPA fixes the cause and prevents it from happening again.
4. Product Complaints, Adverse Event Reporting and Recalls cover problems after the product reaches the market: customer issues, patient safety reports, and pulling a batch back when there is real risk.
5. Supplier Management controls the quality of what comes in, and all six modules feed each other: a complaint can trigger a deviation, which leads to a CAPA, which may change a supplier's status.
