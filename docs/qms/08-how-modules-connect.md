# How the QMS Modules Connect

## The chain
Complaints, adverse events, test results and audit findings raise an alarm. A deviation records and contains the problem. A CAPA fixes the root cause. Supplier management responds if a material was involved. A recall pulls the product back if the risk is wide. Findings feed back into all modules through trend review.

## One story (paracetamol API)
1. Supplier: the lot used a starting material from approved Supplier X.
2. Complaint: a customer reports dark specks.
3. Adverse event: a pharmacist reports liver enzyme rises; the rep forwards it to PV within 24 hours.
4. Deviation: QA opens a deviation; the Production Manager supplies the batch record and holds stock.
5. CAPA: root cause is found; corrective and preventive actions get owners and due dates.
6. Recall: the health-hazard assessment rates it Class I; the lot and downstream batches are recalled.
7. Supplier: if the cause was Supplier X, they get a SCAR and move to conditional status.

## Roles across the chain
- QA Officer / Quality Executive: owns every record, classifies, links, investigates and approves closure.
- Production Manager: supplies batch facts, contains stock and owns shop-floor CAPA actions.

## Link to the CRM
Field reps are often the first to hear about product problems or patient reactions, which is how complaints and adverse events begin.

## One-line summary
The six modules are one connected system: an alarm in one triggers investigation, correction and protection in the others.
